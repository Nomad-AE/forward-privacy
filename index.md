# Forward — Kanban Board Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Data Collection

Forward — Kanban Board does not collect, transmit, or store 
any personal data on external servers. All card data, board 
settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device 
using on-device storage only. No data ever leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

This app does not use analytics, advertising networks, or any 
third-party data collection services.

## In-App Purchases

Forward — Kanban Board offers a one-time purchase to unlock 
full access. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by 
Apple and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not 
receive or store any payment information.

## Children

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13 and 
does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at 
this URL with a revised date.
